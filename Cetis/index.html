About CETIS
Built for the Environmental Toxicology Community

CETIS is a professional-level toxicity data analysis and database application developed by Tidepool Scientific, LLC. Built on the Microsoft Access platform, CETIS thoroughly analyzes, maintains, and organizes complex and heterogeneous toxicity data.

Due to its comprehensive design and time-saving features, CETIS has broad appeal to research and commercial environmental toxicity laboratories, as well as state and federal agencies administering Whole Effluent Toxicity (WET) programs.

Advanced Statistics

Full suite of hypothesis testing and point estimation with 41 non-linear regression models.

Decision-Tree Automation

Automated method selection consistent with EPA, Environment Canada, and other regulatory frameworks.

Multi-User Architecture

File-server architecture with MD5 data integrity, supporting simultaneous access across workstations.

Key Capabilities
Everything You Need for Toxicity Analysis
From data entry to regulatory reporting, CETIS streamlines your entire workflow.

Linear & Non-Linear Regression

4 MLE models and 41 non-linear models including Probit, Logistic, Gompertz, Weibull, and Morgan-Mercer-Flodin.

EPA TST Method

Test for Significant Toxicity using Welch's t Test — a bioequivalence approach controlling the false negative rate.

WET Evaluation Reports

Automated compliance assessment against IWC criteria with full test acceptability validation.

Quality Control Plots

Levey-Jennings (Shewhart), Cumulative Mean, and two-sided CUSUM plots for trend detection.

Regulatory Frameworks

Configurable decision trees for U.S. EPA, Environment Canada, and state-specific requirements.

Split Database Architecture

Front-end application with server-based back-end for secure, multi-user simultaneous access.
