About

The Story Behind CETIS

Since 1992, Tidepool Scientific Software has been committed to developing biostatistical software for the environmental toxicology community.

Our History
From ToxCalc to CETIS

Tidepool Scientific, LLC was founded in McKinleyville, California with a singular mission: to create purpose-built statistical software for environmental toxicology. Our first product, ToxCalc, quickly became the industry standard with over 500 registered users worldwide.

Building on decades of experience and user feedback, we developed CETIS (Comprehensive Environmental Toxicity Information System) as the next evolution. CETIS combines the statistical rigor of ToxCalc with a powerful database architecture, automated decision-tree logic, and comprehensive quality control tools.

Today, CETIS is the most advanced statistical application specifically designed for environmental toxicity testing. It is the only software fully supporting the methodology described in the Guidance Document on Statistical Methods for Environmental Toxicity Tests (Environment Canada, 2005).
